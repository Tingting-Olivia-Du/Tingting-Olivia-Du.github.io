ROCKET aligns a VLA backbone to a 3D vision foundation model through a shared projector and a Matryoshka-style sparse activation scheme, so spatial cues at multiple depths can be distilled into the policy without modality-specific heads at inference. Combined with a training-free layer selection strategy, it reaches 98.5% average success on LIBERO using about 4% of the compute budget of prior state-of-the-art methods, with further gains on LIBERO-Plus and RoboTwin.

Accepted to **NeurIPS 2026**. Code and model weights: [CASE-Lab-UMD/ROCKET-VLA](https://github.com/CASE-Lab-UMD/ROCKET-VLA).
